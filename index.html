A page about cats

Cats are the second most popular household pet in the United States, the only animal ahead of them being dogs, which sit at number one.

Cats are kept as pets in around 46.5 million households in the United States. The average household that keeps cats as pets has two of them.

Three facts about cats

  • Cats cannot taste sweet flavors, because they don't have the taste buds for it.
  • Unlike most humans, cats sleep around 12 to 16 hours a day.
  • Every cat has its own unique nose pattern, much like a human fingerprint.
